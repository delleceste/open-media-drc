How “Which one is playing?” decides

Each version's own page is read and scored against the track on the wire — the one playing, paused, or last played; never the rest of the queue. Every signal that applies adds its points; a comparison one side cannot make adds nothing and is listed with a · instead. The score is clamped to 0–100.
