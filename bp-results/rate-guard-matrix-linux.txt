Reading: the guard reports correctly in both directions and at every bit depth.  Whenever
the decoder rate differs from the chain rate, MPD opens the loopback at the CHAIN's rate —
snd-aloop cannot resample, so MPD converts with its configured resampler (soxr, very high)
before the loopback.  In resamp mode the guard cannot tell a deliberate resample from an
accidental one.
